Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Applies to the ENGINEX Services Android app, iPhone/iPad home-screen web app and service-request website.

Who operates this service

ENGINEX Engineering Services is the service brand managed by Faraz Nawaz. For privacy questions or requests, email faraznawaz2014@gmail.com or contact +966 55 167 9504.

Information you provide

When you submit a service enquiry, we collect your name, phone number, email address, city or neighbourhood, service description and any property, quantity, timing and urgency details you choose to provide. You may optionally upload fault photos. Messages sent through request tracking and associated quotations, visit arrangements and completion records are stored with the request.

Do not submit identity documents, payment-card details, medical records or unrelated sensitive personal information. Photo uploads are optional.

How the information is used

We use the information to review enquiries, prepare quotations, arrange maintenance, communicate with you, track the request and maintain related service records. Each saved request receives a tracking reference. The tracking screen requires the reference and the submitted email address.

Photo and device access

The Android app uses the system file picker so you can choose images. It does not request broad storage, camera, contacts or device-location permissions. The city/area is typed by you; the app does not collect GPS location. Optional photo optimization happens on your device before upload.

Hosting and service providers

The website is delivered through GitHub Pages. Request data and uploaded files are processed and stored through the ENGINEX backend hosted using OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare infrastructure. Hosting providers process network information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the service. The backend uses a daily IP-derived hash to limit abuse. If an email sender is configured, notification messages are processed by the configured email provider, currently supported through Resend.

These providers may process data outside your country. Call, email and WhatsApp buttons open your selected applications, whose privacy practices apply when you use them. We do not sell your request data or use it for advertising. The Android app contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Access and security

Customer records and uploaded job photos are restricted to the authenticated ENGINEX owner dashboard. The public tracker returns the request's customer-visible status, shared quotation, visit details and messages after the reference and email match. Private owner notes and technician assignments are excluded from the tracker. Connections use HTTPS. Do not share your reference and email combination with people you do not want accessing the tracking information.

Retention, correction and deletion

Service records are retained for handling enquiries, follow-up and related operational records. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing faraznawaz2014@gmail.com. Include the request reference and the email originally submitted so we can identify the record. We may ask for reasonable verification and explain if any records need to be retained. There is no customer account to create or delete.

Children

The app is intended for adults arranging home or business maintenance. It is not designed for children.

Changes

This page will be updated if the service's data practices change. The effective date appears above.
